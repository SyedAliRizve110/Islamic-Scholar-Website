Authoritative Islamic Jurisprudence & Religious Service

Dedicated to elucidating the divine commands and answering everyday religious inquiries.

This portal serves as the official online depository of answered religious questions and practical jurisprudence by Ayatullah Syed Sibtain Abbas Rizvi. In the tradition of the school of Ahlulbayt (a.s.), believers rely on the scholarly deduction of qualified jurists (Fuqaha) who derive legal rulings (Ahkam) through rigorous study of the Holy Quran, the prophetic Sunnah, and the traditions of the infallible Imams (a.s.).

Inquiries received through direct scholarly consultations, correspondence, and digital submissions are meticulously analyzed, answered, and cataloged into an open, searchable index for the enduring benefit of Muslims worldwide. Whether inquiring about daily acts of worship, commercial contracts, marriage and inheritance, or contemporary issues like digital currency, believers can readily access authoritative answers.

Core Jurisprudential Topics & Practice Areas

Browse categorized rulings across the primary branches of Islamic jurisprudence (Ahkam al-Shar'iyyah):

Acts of Worship (Ibadat)

Comprehensive legal rulings on Daily Prayers (Salah), Ritual Purity (Taharah), Ablution (Wudhu & Ghusl), Fasting in the Holy Month of Ramadan (Sawm), and Pilgrimage (Hajj & Umrah).

Financial Dues & Charity

Precise calculation guidelines and payment obligations for Khums (Sahm-e-Imam and Sahm-e-Sadat), Zakat, recommended charity (Sadaqah), and fulfilling conditional vows (Nadhr).

Commercial Law & Trade

Halal business transactions, prohibition of usury (Riba), modern banking, online commerce, cryptocurrency, stock investments, debts, and fair contract requirements.

Family Law & Estate

Rulings governing Islamic Marriage (Nikah), bridal gift (Mahr), parental guardianship, divorce procedures (Talaq), child custody, and distribution of inheritance (Mirth) and wills.
