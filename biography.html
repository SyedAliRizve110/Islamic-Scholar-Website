1970 – Early Studies

Early Life & Seminary Studies in India

Ayatullah Syed Sibtain Abbas Rizvi was born in 1970 in Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh, India. From his youth, he dedicated himself to sacred knowledge and commenced his preliminary and intermediate Islamic seminary studies at Anwarul Uloom, Allahabad.

During this foundational period in India, he pursued Islamic jurisprudence, theology, and Arabic grammar under the guidance of renowned scholars:

  • Syed Allama Jawadi
  • Syed Ahsan Abbas Rizvi
Hawza Ilmiyya Qum

Advanced Studies in Qum, Iran

To attain higher stages of jurisprudence (Fiqh) and legal methodology (Usul al-Fiqh), he migrated to the historic seminary center of Qum, Islamic Republic of Iran.

In Qum, he attended the advanced post-graduate lectures (Dars-e-Kharij) and ethical discourses delivered by some of the most prominent authorities and luminaries of the era, including:

  • Ayatollah Muhammad-Taqi Bahjat (r.a.)
  • Ayatollah Hassan Hasanzadeh Amoli (r.a.)
  • And other esteemed jurists and professors of the Hawza
Scholarly Rank

Attainment of Ijtihad

Through sustained research, deep mastery of Quranic sciences, Hadith traditions, and principles of jurisprudence, Ayatullah Syed Sibtain Abbas Rizvi successfully attained the rank of Ijtihad—the scholarly competence to derive independent legal rulings from primary Islamic sources.

Present Activity

Residence & Religious Service

Ayatullah Syed Sibtain Abbas Rizvi divides his time between Qum, Iran and India.

His work is dedicated to religious education, guidance, and community service for the followers of Ahlulbayt (a.s.), including answering religious inquiries, teaching seminary students, and providing rulings on contemporary jurisprudential challenges.
